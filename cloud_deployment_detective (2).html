Key takeaways

  • Public cloud: cloud services are offered to many customers over a network, usually the internet.
  • Private cloud: cloud infrastructure is dedicated to one organisation.
  • Hybrid cloud: combines private and public cloud environments.
  • Community cloud: infrastructure is shared by organisations with common needs, such as similar security or compliance requirements.
